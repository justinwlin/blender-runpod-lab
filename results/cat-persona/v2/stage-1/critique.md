# Stage 1 — mirrored Skin cage

Viewed front, side, top and three-quarter together with the supplied concept. The first cage had a narrow diamond head, shallow profile and long-looking neck. Revisions widened the two head rings, deepened their radii and broadened the lower cheek/neck transition. The broad head now exceeds the shoulders and the body remains small. Mirror precedes Skin and Subdivision on one connected stick skeleton.

Gate: proceed to fusion. This is an intentionally low-poly anatomical cage, not a finished character. The planar chin and angular ear/cheek transitions still need the continuous remesh and sculpt passes. The floor contact will be checked on the applied skin rather than inferred from the soft preview shadow. No fur, color or accessories have been added.
