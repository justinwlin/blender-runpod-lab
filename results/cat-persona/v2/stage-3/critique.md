# Stage 3 — primary, secondary, tertiary sculpt

Viewed all four clay views beside the concept. The first secondary pass made the jowls too ridged; softened Gaussian muzzle and cheek falloffs remove those creases. Primary edits broaden/round the skull without replacing it with a sphere. Secondary edits push a short muzzle and paw/toe volume from the connected skin. Tertiary edits add restrained surface variation and polish.

Gate: proceed to clean-quads retopology. The standing proportions and feline ears read, and the paws have forward volume in profile. The jaw still needs a human sculpt pass for exact reference anatomy; facial expression must be evaluated with socketed, flattened eyes at the look stage. This is a neutral sculpt, not a final preview.
