# Stage 2 — fused skin

Viewed all four neutral-clay contact views against the concept. Fusion removes the cage facets and makes head, neck, shoulders, arms and legs read as one continuous surface. No stacked primitive seams remain. The broad head and tiny torso are retained; the profile still lacks a feline muzzle and the feet are too capsule-like.

Gate: proceed to the three sculpt passes. The next pass must round the forehead/jowls, push the muzzle from the existing skin, broaden the planted paws and add cheek silhouette variation. It must preserve the connected shoulder and hip transitions. This clay gate is not a finished look.
